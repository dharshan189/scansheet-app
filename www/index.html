About Multi-Export

Simultaneous multi-format conversion & packaging

Multi-Export is ScanSheet's batch conversion feature that lets you export your scanned tables into multiple file formats simultaneously with a single click.

Instead of converting your table file-by-file or relying on separate third-party software, Multi-Export packages all desired formats together in seconds.

📊
Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)
Fully structured spreadsheets with preserved rows, columns, and numeric formats ready for data analysis.
📝
Microsoft Word (.docx)
Cleanly formatted document tables ready for business reports, documentation, and office printing.
📄
PDF Document (.pdf)
Vector-quality PDF with crisp table borders, headers, and professional layout.
🖼️
High-Resolution Image (.png)
Lossless graphic preview suitable for embedding into slide decks, chats, or presentations.
📦
ZIP Archive Bundle
Compresses and bundles all generated files into a single convenient archive for instant download or sharing.
